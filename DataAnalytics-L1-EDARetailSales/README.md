# Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of retail sales data to identify sales trends, customer behaviour, product category performance, and business insights.

The analysis uses Python libraries to explore datasets and create meaningful visualizations that support better business decision-making.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Understand retail sales patterns.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Study customer demographics based on age and gender.
- Identify revenue generated by different product categories.
- Analyze the top 10 best-selling products.
- Understand relationships between numerical variables using correlation analysis.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 3. Datasets Used

**Dataset 1: Retail Sales Dataset**

Source: [Kaggle – Retail Sales Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohammadtalib786/retail-sales-dataset)

Contains 1,000 transactions with information such as:
- Transaction ID
- Date
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount

**Dataset 2: Sample Superstore Dataset**

Source: [Kaggle – Sample Superstore Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)

Used separately for:
- Top 10 best-selling products by total sales revenue.
- Product-level sales analysis.

**Note:** Both datasets are analyzed separately. They are not merged because they contain different records and data structures.

## 6. Analysis Performed

### A. Data Inspection
- Loaded and inspected datasets.
- Checked data types and missing values.
- Checked duplicate records.
- The Retail Sales Dataset contained 1,000 records and 9 columns, with no missing or duplicate records.

### B. Descriptive Statistics
Calculated:
- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard Deviation

### C. Time Series Analysis
- Monthly retail sales trends.
- Quarterly retail sales trends.
- Created line charts to understand sales fluctuations.

### D. Customer Demographics
- Customer age group distribution.
- Gender distribution.
- Average transaction value by gender.

### E. Product Category Analysis
- Revenue by product category.
- Quantity sold by category.

### F. Top 10 Best-Selling Products
- Used the Sample Superstore Dataset.
- Ranked the top 10 products based on total sales revenue.
- Created a horizontal bar chart.

### G. Correlation Analysis
- Calculated the correlation matrix.
- Created a heatmap to understand relationships between numerical variables.

## 7. Key Findings

- May 2023 recorded the highest monthly sales in the Retail Sales Dataset.
- Q4 2023 recorded the highest quarterly sales.
- Customers aged 41–50 formed the largest age group.
- Electronics generated the highest revenue among the three product categories.
- Price per Unit and Total Amount showed a strong positive correlation of approximately 0.85.
- Female and male customers had almost equal average transaction values.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier recorded the highest sales revenue among the selected top 10 products in the Superstore Dataset.

## 8. Business Recommendations

1. Maintain sufficient inventory for high-revenue product categories.
2. Develop suitable marketing campaigns for major customer age groups.
3. Plan promotional activities according to monthly and quarterly sales patterns.
4. Monitor product pricing and quantity to understand their effect on transaction revenue.
5. Explore promotional offers to improve sales in lower-revenue categories.

## 9. Dataset Limitations

- The Retail Sales Dataset does not contain individual product names. Therefore, product-level Top 10 analysis was performed separately using the Sample Superstore Dataset.
- The two datasets were not merged.
- January 2024 contains a small amount of sales data and may represent a partial month, so comparisons with full months should be interpreted carefully.

## 10. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Exploratory Data Analysis can help understand retail sales performance, customer demographics, product categories, and sales patterns.

The visualizations and findings provide useful insights that can support inventory planning, marketing strategies, and business decisions.
